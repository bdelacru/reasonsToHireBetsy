ReasonsToHireBetsy v2: homepage concepts

Exploratory prototypes. None of these is an approved direction, and their colors, fonts, layouts and interactions aren’t production standards. All three follow the approved progressive personality reveal: engineering first, photography further down. Write-up: docs/research/v2-homepage-concept-comparison.md.

  • A · Colorful Editorial

    Magazine-style: large serif headlines, a contents strip, strong color blocks. Photos enter as a full-bleed interlude after Experience.

    Open Concept A

  • B · Creative Scrapbook

    Paper, tape, stickers and notebook pages. Professional content stays on straight sheets; playful layering happens around it. Photos arrive as flippable prints.

    Open Concept B

  • C · Interactive Engineering Studio

    A dark hero with an interactive system-layer explorer, then light sections on a signal rail. Photos appear as a three-stage “pipeline”.

    Open Concept C
