01

Approach

I tend to ask one more question.

A feature request is usually the beginning of the conversation, not the end of it. Before I build, I want to understand what someone is actually trying to accomplish.

The ticket says

“Add search.”

  1. What are users actually trying to find?
  2. Which fields should be searchable?
  3. Exact, partial, or case-insensitive matching?
  4. Would filters communicate intent more clearly?
  5. Can someone use it fully with a keyboard?
  6. What happens when nothing matches?

Good engineering starts before implementation.
